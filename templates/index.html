Connecting your Wii & a few details
  1. Keep this computer on and connect the Wii to the same LAN.
  2. Back up the Wii’s existing apps/wiimc/onlinemedia.xml.
  3. Save this onlinemedia.xml beside WiiMC’s boot.dol. If your app is in apps/WiiMC-SS, put it there and in apps/wiimc.
  4. Restart WiiMC-SS, choose Online Media → RiiTube at Home → Prepared videos and progress.

To keep your other providers, add this folder inside your existing XML’s <file> element instead:

<folder name="RiiTube at Home">
  <link name="Search YouTube" addr="{{ base }}/wiimc/search.plx?q=" type="search" />
  <link name="Prepared videos and progress" addr="{{ base }}/wiimc/library.plx" type="playlist" />
</folder>

Videos are prepared at up to 360p / 30 fps and kept on this computer. Preparation downloads the whole video first, so longer videos need more time and disk space.
